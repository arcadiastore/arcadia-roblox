# 27 — Sistem Audio

## Ringkasan

Audio adalah elemen penting yang menciptakan atmosfer dan immersion. Sistem audio mencakup BGM (Background Music), SFX (Sound Effects), dan Voice.

## BGM (Background Music)

### Area BGM
| Area | Gama Musik | Mood |
|------|------------|------|
| Beginner Village | Akustik, flute | Damai, hangat |
| Green Forest | Orchestral, strings | Misterius, alami |
| Sage Tower | Piano, ambient | Mistis, tenang |
| Capital City | March, brass | Megah, sibuk |
| Dark Caverns | Low strings, choir | Gelap, menakutkan |
| Sky Islands | Harp, choir | Epik, bebas |

### Situasi BGM
| Situasi | Gama Musik |
|---------|------------|
| Pertarungan normal | Tempo cepat, energik |
| Pertarungan boss | Epik, dramatis |
| Cutscene | Emosional, cinematic |
| Menu | Tenang, ambient |
| Victory | Ceria, triumphant |

## SFX (Sound Effects)

### Pertarungan
| Aksi | Suara |
|------|-------|
| Serangan fisik | Slash, impact |

| Critical hit | Amplified impact |
| Dodge | Swift movement |
| Block | Shield clang |
| Heal | Chime, sparkle |
| Level up | Fanfare |
| Death | Dramatic fall |

### Eksplorasi
| Aksi | Suara |
|------|-------|

### UI
| Aksi | Suara |
|------|-------|

## Voice

### Voice Acting
- **NPC Dialog:** Text only (tanpa voice)
- **Battle Cry:** Voice saat skill kuat
- **Boss:** Voice saat fase baru
- **Cutscene:** Voice untuk cutscene penting

### Voice Volume
- Adjustable: 0% - 100%
- Separate slider per category

## Audio Settings

| Setting | Range | Default |
|---------|-------|---------|
| Master Volume | 0-100% | 80% |
| BGM Volume | 0-100% | 70% |
| SFX Volume | 0-100% | 80% |
| Voice Volume | 0-100% | 80% |
| Ambient Volume | 0-100% | 60% |

## Audio Priority

1. **Voice** — Prioritas tertinggi
2. **SFX** — Prioritas tinggi
3. **BGM** — Prioritas medium
4. **Ambient** — Prioritas rendah
