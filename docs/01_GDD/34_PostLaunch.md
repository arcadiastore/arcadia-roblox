# 34 — Pasca Rilis (Post Launch)

## Ringkasan

Rencana pasca rilis mengatur konten dan update yang akan ditambahkan setelah game dirilis. Tujuan menjaga pemain tetap terlibat dan game tetap relevan.

## Strategi Pasca Rilis

### 1. Konten Berkala
- Update konten setiap 2-3 bulan
- Konten baru: dungeon, quest, boss, area
- Event musiman

### 2. Komunitas
- Feedback loop dengan pemain
- Community events
- Developer stream

### 3. Monetisasi (MMORPG)
- Cosmetic shop
- Battle pass
- Expansion pack

## Rencana Update

### Update 1: "Awakening" (+3 bulan)
- 2 dungeon baru
- 1 job baru
- 10 quest sampingan
- Sistem pet
- Balance patch

### Update 2: "Conflict" (+6 bulan)
- 1 area baru
- War Battle system
- Guild system
- 20 quest baru
- PvP arena

### Update 3: "Darkness" (+9 bulan)
- 3 dungeon baru
- World boss
- Raid system
- 30 quest baru
- Legendary equipment

### Update 4: "Revelation" (+12 bulan)
- Expansion: Cerita baru
- 2 area baru
- Job Tier 3 untuk semua
- Level cap: 120
- Endgame content

## Battle Pass

### Struktur
- **Tier:** 50 tier per season
- **Durasi:** 3 bulan per season
- **Reward:** Costume, mount, pet, material
- **Free Track:** 20 tier gratis
- **Premium Track:** 50 tier (bayar)

### Reward Contoh
| Tier | Free | Premium |
|------|------|---------|
| 5 | Potion x10 | Costume set |
| 10 | Gold 1000 | Mount skin |
| 20 | Material pack | Pet |
| 30 | Enhancement Stone | Weapon skin |
| 50 | Title | Legendary costume |

## Cosmetic Shop

### Jenis Item
| Item | Harga (Diamond) |
|------|-----------------|
| Costume | 100-500 |
| Mount skin | 200-800 |
| Pet skin | 150-600 |
| Emote | 50-100 |
| Title | 100-300 |

### Prinsip
- **Tidak pay-to-win** — Hanya kosmetik
- **Fair pricing** — Harga wajar
- **No lootbox** — Beli langsung
