# Armor (Art Bible)

## Ringkasan

Panduan visual untuk desain armor.

## Kategori Armor

### Heavy Armor
- **Job:** Warrior, Knight, Berserker
- **Ciri:** Tebal, penuh, plate armor
- **Warna:** Silver, grey, dark metal
- **Detail:** Engraving, cape, horn

### Light Armor
- **Job:** Archer, Ranger, Assassin
- **Ciri:** Ringan, fleksibel, leather
- **Warna:** Coklat, hijau, hitam
- **Detail:** Belt, pouch, hood

### Cloth Armor
- **Job:** Mage, Wizard, Cleric
- **Ciri:** Robe panjang, flowing
- **Warna:** Biru, putih, ungu, merah
- **Detail:** Rune, embroidery, gem

## Armor Slot Visual

### Head
- **Helmet:** Full face, half face, crown
- **Hat:** Wizard hat, hood, headband
- **Mask:** Assassin mask, plague doctor

### Body
- **Plate:** Full plate, half plate
- **Leather:** Vest, jacket, tunic
- **Robe:** Long robe, short robe

### Hands
- **Gauntlet:** Full metal, half metal
- **Gloves:** Leather, cloth
- **Bracer:** Arm guard

### Feet
- **Boots:** Heavy boots, light boots
- **Shoes:** Cloth shoes, sandals
- **Greaves:** Leg armor
